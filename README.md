# Mimic

Your D&D character sheet, on your phone. It looks like the printed 2014 sheet, and it tracks what changes at the
table: hit points, conditions, exhaustion, spell slots, and features with limited uses.

Open it at **https://evanmassi.github.io/mimic/**

## What it does

- Keeps your HP at the top of the screen at all times. Drag the bar left for damage, right for healing, or tap it to
  unroll quick steps (−10 to +10) and an amount box that shows where Damage, Heal or Temp HP would leave you. Temp HP
  comes off first.
- Shows conditions, exhaustion and spell slots at the top too. Death saves appear when you drop to 0.
- Chapter tabs under the HUD (Stats, Skills, Attacks, Spells, Features, Gear, Notes) jump around the long sheet and
  underline where you are.
- Works out modifiers, saves, skills, passive perception, attack bonuses and spell DC from your scores and level.
- Things that change every session are always one tap: coins, equipment, languages, attacks, spells, max HP.
  Things that only change on level-up (scores, proficiencies, class details) sit behind the Edit quill so a stray
  tap can't change them.
- Spell slots are sapphires, a shade deeper at each level, that you tap to spend or get back, each one on its own.
- Tracks features: uses that come back on a rest, pools like Lay on Hands (tap the vial to heal yourself, heal an
  ally or cure, with a preview of where each leaves you), switches like a Tortle's Shell Defense (a shell you tap to withdraw, with the +4 shown on your AC)
  that change your AC and speed, and Divine Smite buttons that spend a slot.
- Short and long rests bring back what they should.
- Backup, folded at the very bottom, saves your character, session and notes to one small file (the share sheet on
  a phone, a download on a computer) and loads one back after showing you what it would replace.
- Undo, at the top, takes back the last few changes that are hard to redo by hand: damage, healing, a Smite,
  a rest. Taps you can simply tap again, like conditions and orbs, don't need it.
- Has a spellbook of nearly 2,000 openly licensed spells (both SRDs plus third-party and homebrew books, each
  labeled). Tap a spell to read it, pick which version you use, swap it for another, or add new ones. Spells from
  books that aren't openly licensed can be added by name, with your own text.
- Has a notes box you can type in or draw on.

## Put it on your phone

- **iPhone:** open the link in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.
- **Android:** open the link in Chrome, tap the menu, then Add to Home screen.

## Your data

Everything stays on your phone. There's no account and nothing gets uploaded. Clearing your browser's website data
erases it.
